# Power BI Dashboards

This repository contains three interactive Power BI dashboards developed to analyze business data, identify trends, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), and support data-driven decision-making.

## 1. HR Attrition Dashboard
**Objective:** To analyze employee attrition, understand workforce trends, and identify factors influencing employee turnover.

**Key Insights:**
- Analyzes attrition across departments, age groups, job roles, and other employee characteristics.
- Identifies employee segments with higher attrition rates.
- Helps HR managers understand workforce patterns and potential retention challenges.

**Business Outcome:** Supports HR teams in developing employee retention strategies, improving workforce planning, and reducing recruitment costs.

## 2. Sales Dashboard
**Objective:** To evaluate sales performance, monitor profitability, and identify opportunities for business growth.

**Key Insights:**
- Tracks sales, profit, orders, and other key performance indicators.
- Compares performance across product categories, customer segments, and regions.
- Identifies high-performing and underperforming business areas.

**Business Outcome:** Helps management optimize sales strategies, improve product decisions, allocate resources effectively, and increase profitability.

## 3. Supply Chain Dashboard
**Objective:** To analyze supply chain performance and identify opportunities for improving operational efficiency.

**Key Insights:**
- Evaluates supply chain activities, logistics performance, and operational trends.
- Highlights potential inefficiencies and areas requiring improvement.
- Supports analysis of distribution and resource utilization.

**Business Outcome:** Helps supply chain managers improve operational planning, optimize logistics decisions, and identify potential cost-saving opportunities.

## Tools & Skills Used
- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPI analysis
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships between tables
- **Business Analytics** – Translating data into actionable management insights

## Project Outcome
These dashboards demonstrate the practical application of business intelligence to HR, Sales, and Supply Chain Management, transforming raw data into meaningful insights for better strategic and operational decisions.
